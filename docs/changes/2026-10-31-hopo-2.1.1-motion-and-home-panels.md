# 2026-10-31: hopo 2.1.1, motion and Home panels from the design

Owner's request: 2.1.0 left out the motion in the design canvas ([Design](https://claude.ai/artifact/PETTHAnoGBq6XJZPnP68ko)). Implement, from the design files:
- every animation
- the button sizes and button animation
- the Home panels that rise from the dock
- the floating wave rings

Values below are taken from the design's CSS and script (Main, Menu and the page files). Every animation is off when the system asks for reduced motion (Windows: animations off; macOS/Linux: the equivalent setting where available), and in preview renders, which show the settled state.

## Items

- [ ] 1. **Drifting glows on every page.**
  - The purple glow drifts to translate(-14%, 10%) scale(.95) over 60 s.
  - The green glow drifts to translate(18%, 12%) scale(1.1) over 68 s.
  - Both ease in and out and reverse forever.
- [ ] 2. **Spring buttons.**
  - Hover: scale 1.02 with a 0.3° tilt, alternating -0.3° and +0.3° between neighbouring buttons. The transition is 0.45 s with a slight overshoot (cubic-bezier(.25,1.12,.5,1)).
  - Press: scale .97 in 0.15 s.
  - The icon wiggles on hover: rotate to ∓6° and settle at ∓4° over 0.6 s.
  - The primary hover colour is `#FFC567`.
  - Buttons are 44 px tall with 12 px corners. Small buttons are 34 px with 10 px corners.
- [ ] 3. **Living glass cards.**
  - A purple-to-mint line along the top edge (18% inset each side, with a soft glow) slides ±22% and brightens from .7 to 1 over 7 s, reversing forever.
  - A diagonal light sweeps across the card edge every 9 s.
- [ ] 4. **Page entrance.** Each top-level block of a page rises 16 px and fades in over 0.7 s (cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)), with blocks 0.08 s apart.
- [ ] 5. **Menu motion.**
  - The scrim fades in over 0.4 s.
  - The panel grows from the menu button (scale .1 to 1, 0.58 s, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), fully visible by 40%).
  - Rows fade up 6 px over 0.5 s, starting at 0.12 s and 0.04 s apart.
  - Row hover: shift 2 px right; the icon tile tilts ±4° and scales 1.04.
- [ ] 6. **Home field.**
  - **12 wave rings** outside the inner rings. Each ring has:
    - a radius R (268–299 px on the 640 px stage), wave height A (5–12) and lobe count n (10–30) from the design's table
    - one colour: violet, cyan, mint, pink, periwinkle and their tints
    - a 0.55 px glowing stroke
    - a slow spin (75–240 s, half of them reversed)
    - a morph through three shapes (A, 1.35 A and 0.5 A, with shifted phase) every 5.5–12 s
    - a shimmer between .55 and 1 opacity every 7 s
  - Moving the pointer near the rings slows them (rate 1 − .75 × proximity) and swells or shrinks each ring slightly. Proximity is 1 at radius 284 and falls to 0 at ±110 px.
  - **Water ripples:** two glass circles (200 px) grow from the centre (scale .6 to 2.1, opacity peaking at .55) every 12 s, 6 s apart.
  - **Device dots:**
    - PCs are mint `#3DF5A0` and phones are violet `#B57CFF`, each with a halo.
    - Dots blink between .4 and 1 opacity and .85 and 1 scale every 3.8 s, with a radar ping ring (scale .6 to 2.6, fading out) on the same beat.
    - Each device starts at a different point of the beat.
  - **Device card:** hovering a device (or giving it keyboard focus) replaces its name tag with a glass card that rises 8 px from scale .97 over 0.45 s. The card shows:
    - the name and a status chip
    - the address and trust line
    - Send files and Text (PCs), or Send files and Disconnect (phones)
  - The stage scales down on short or narrow windows.
- [ ] 7. **Home dock and rising panels.**
  - The dock rises 26 px and fades in (0.9 s, after 0.15 s). It has four buttons:
    - Send files (primary)
    - Folder
    - Text
    - Phone
  - Each button toggles a 440 px glass panel above the dock. The panel grows out of its button (squash and settle): from 26 px lower at scale .62 × .5 with a 3 px blur, to slightly past full size at 55%, then to rest, over 0.6 s. Its contents fade up 6 px after 0.14 s.
  - The open button shows its "on" state. Clicking outside closes the panel.
  - **Send files panel:**
    - a dashed drop area ("Drop files here" with Choose files) that shows the current selection
    - "Send to" chips for paired PCs (mint dot) and approved phones (violet dot); the chosen one is highlighted in violet
    - a Send button that starts the real transfer
  - **Folder panel:** choose a folder, pick Read only or Read & upload, then Start sharing to create the shared space. It also links to all shared folders.
  - **Text panel:** a message box, "Send to" chips for paired PCs plus "This PC (for my phone)", and Share.
  - **Phone panel:** the QR code and address with "Same Wi‑Fi, then scan with your camera.", or Start browser sharing when it is off.
  - Send files and Text on a device card open these panels with that device chosen.
- [ ] 8. **macOS/Linux app.** Items 1–7 in Avalonia.
- [ ] 9. **Phone page.** Button spring and icon wiggle, card edge sweep and top line, and the staggered entrance, matching items 2–4.
